📜 ARCHERS CHESS OFFICIAL RULES (v1.0)

Based on the official declaration by John Patrick Read (3 Jan 2026).

1. The Archer Piece ($A$)

The Archer is a ranged tactical piece valued at approx. 4 points. It moves in two mandatory phases:

  • Outward Phase ($O$): Exactly three squares in a straight or diagonal direction[cite: 27].
  • Return Phase ($F$): One square back toward origin at a 45° diagonal offset[cite: 31, 32].

Blocking: Straight moves cannot jump over pieces; diagonal moves can[cite: 28, 29, 103, 112]. Captures only occur on the final square[cite: 25, 34].

2. The Board and Setup

Played on a 10 × 8 board[cite: 19]. Files: –A, A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, H+[cite: 19]. Setup includes two Archers per side and two additional pawns[cite: 22].

3. Standard Rules

All standard chess rules apply, including castling, promotion, and en passant[cite: 17, 40].
