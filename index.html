Official Rules of Play

1. The Archer Piece (A):

Moves exactly 3 squares in a straight or diagonal direction (Outward Phase), followed by a mandatory 1-square diagonal return step (Final Phase)[cite: 24, 27, 31]. Captures and Checks only occur on the final square[cite: 33, 35]. Straight moves cannot jump pieces; diagonals can[cite: 28, 29].

2. Board Layout:

Played on a 10x8 board with files -A through H+. Standard chess rules (Castling, En Passant) apply unchanged[cite: 40].

3. Scoring System:
  • Win: 3 Points | Draw: 1 Point.
  • Piece Value: Archer is valued at 4 points.
