Official Rules of Play [cite: 11]

The Archer Piece ($A$)[cite: 23, 56]:

A tactical ranged piece moving in two phases: 3 squares outward ($O$) and 1 diagonal return step ($F$)[cite: 24, 57, 58]. Captures and checks only occur on the final square ($F$)[cite: 25, 33, 35]. Straight moves are blocked by pieces; diagonal moves can jump[cite: 28, 29, 112].

The Board[cite: 18]:

Played on a $10\times8$ grid. Files: -A, A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, H+[cite: 19, 61].

Scoring & Victory:
  • Win: 3 Points | Draw: 1 Point[cite: 40].
  • Checkmate: Standard chess rules apply[cite: 36, 134].
Piece Values[cite: 41]:

Archer: 4 | Rook: 5 | Bishop/Knight: 3 | Pawn: 1.
