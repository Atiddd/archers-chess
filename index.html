Official Rules [cite: 11]

The Archer ($A$): Moves 3 squares outward ($O$) and 1 diagonal return step ($F$). Captures/Checks ONLY on $F$[cite: 24, 25, 34, 35]. Straight moves are blocked; diagonals jump[cite: 28, 29]. Board is $10\times8$.

Scoring: Win = 3pts, Draw = 1pt.
