📜 OFFICIAL RULES & MOVEMENT APPENDICES

1. The Archer Piece ($A$)

The Archer is a ranged piece (Value: 4) moving in two phases:

  • Outward ($O$): Exactly 3 squares (Straight or Diagonal).
  • Return ($F$): Exactly 1 square back toward origin at a 45° offset.
Note: Straight outward moves are blocked by pieces. Diagonal outward moves may jump.

2. The 10x8 Field of Play

Files: –A, A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, H+. Ranks: 1–8.

3. Victory Conditions

Check: Occurs when a King is under attack (including by an Archer's final square).
Checkmate: Occurs when a King is in Check and has no legal means of escape.
