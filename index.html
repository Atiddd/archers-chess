Official Rules of Play

1. The Archer Piece ($A$)

The Archer is a ranged tactical piece valued at approximately 4 points[cite: 42]. It moves in two mandatory phases[cite: 24]:

  • Outward Move: Exactly 3 squares in a straight or diagonal direction[cite: 27]. Straight moves cannot jump pieces; diagonals can[cite: 28, 29].
  • Return Step: From the 3rd square, it must move exactly 1 square back toward its origin at a $45^{\circ}$ diagonal offset[cite: 31].
  • Capture: Only occurs on the final landing square ($F$)[cite: 34].

2. The Field of Play

Played on a $10\times8$ board. Files are labeled: –A, A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, H+[cite: 19]. All standard chess rules (Castling, En Passant, Promotion) apply unchanged[cite: 40].

3. Victory Conditions

The Archer gives check only from its final square[cite: 36]. The game ends in Checkmate when a King is under attack and has no legal escape. Standard stalemate rules apply[cite: 36].
