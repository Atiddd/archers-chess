📜 Game Rules & Archer Movement (click to collapse)

Archers Chess is played on a 10×8 board with standard chess rules + 2 Archers per side.

The Archer moves in two mandatory phases:

  • Outward: exactly 3 squares straight or diagonal (straight: no jump; diagonal: can jump).
  • Return: exactly 1 square back at 45° diagonal (choose left or right).
  • Capture only on the final square.

Castling works as in standard chess (king + rook, path clear, not in check).

Tile labels match official notation: files –A to H+ (left to right from White’s view), ranks 1–8.
