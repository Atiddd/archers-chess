📜 OFFICIAL RULES & MOVEMENT APPENDICES

1. The Archer Piece ($A$)

Movement Phases: Outward ($O$) 3 squares; Return ($F$) 1 square diagonal offset. The King cannot be captured; the game ends only by Checkmate or Stalemate.

2. Notation & Layout

Files: –A, A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, H+. AI plays at "Normal" difficulty.
