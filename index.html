Official Rules of Play

The Archer Piece (A):

A tactical ranged piece moving in two phases: exactly 3 squares outward (O) in a straight or diagonal line, followed by a mandatory 1-square diagonal return step (F). Captures and checks only occur on the final square. Diagonal outward moves may jump pieces; straight outward moves may not.

The Board:

Played on a 10x8 grid. Files are: -A, A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, H+. Ranks are numbered 1 to 8.

Standard Rules:

All standard chess rules apply unchanged, including castling, en passant, promotion, and draw conditions. Standard pawns promote normally.

Piece Values:

Archer: 4 | Rook: 5 | Bishop: 3 | Knight: 3 | Pawn: 1.
