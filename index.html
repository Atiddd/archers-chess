Official Rules of Play

The Archer Piece (A):

A tactical ranged piece moving in two phases: 3 squares outward (O) and 1 diagonal return step (F). Captures and checks ONLY occur on the final square (F). Straight moves are blocked by pieces; diagonal moves can jump.

The Board:

Played on a 10x8 grid. Files: -A, A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, H+.

Scoring & Victory:
  • Win: 3 Points | Draw: 1 Point.
  • Checkmate: Standard chess rules apply. Castling is active.
Piece Values:

Archer: 4 | Rook: 5 | Bishop/Knight: 3 | Pawn: 1.
